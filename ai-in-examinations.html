AI in Examinations

Examinations run on tight deadlines and zero tolerance for error. Instead of relying solely on the legacy ERP or asking for more people and budget, also deployed agentic AI to remove the bottlenecks directly.

The approach

Diagnose

Find where results, forms and printing slow down or go wrong, and who it affects.

Build in-house

Design a fix with a student team under her mentorship, as an AI-native tool rather than an ERP add-on.

Close the gap

Ship it into the live exam cycle, with analytics to watch it and a payment gateway where fees apply.

Dashboards and tools launched

  1. Exams Monitoring & Evaluation Dashboard

    One view of examination conduct and evaluation progress across the university.

  2. Final Question-Paper Set Selection

    Choosing the final question-paper set that goes for printing.

  3. Result Validation

    Checks on results before they are formally declared to students.

  4. Special Examination Form Filling

    Online applications for special examinations, with integrated payment.

  5. Convocation Form Filling

    Online convocation registration for graduating students.
